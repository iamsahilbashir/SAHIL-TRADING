About Sahil Trading

Trading education without the hype.

A beginner-focused platform built around clear explanations, chart study, risk management and disciplined practice.

The mission

Learn. Practise. Improve.

Sahil Trading is designed to make forex education easier to follow. The focus is on understanding what is happening on a chart and developing a process that can be studied and tested.

The 30-Day Trading Series gives beginners a structured starting point, while the website can grow into a library of lessons, videos, notes and resources.

SimpleClear explanations without unnecessary complexity.
PracticalConcepts connected to chart study and examples.
Risk-firstCapital protection is part of the learning process.
